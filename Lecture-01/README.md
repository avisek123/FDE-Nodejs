# Lecture 01: Introduction to Forward Deployed Engineering & AI Chatbots

## 📖 Overview
Lecture 1 introduces the role and responsibilities of a **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** and examines how enterprise customer support chatbots have evolved.

### Key Concepts Covered:
1. **The FDE Role**:
   - Working at the intersection of core software engineering, client deployment, and LLM application design.
   - Diagnosing real customer problems and shipping production-grade AI solutions.
2. **Evolution of Chatbot Systems**:
   - **Rule-Based Systems**: Keyword / regex-driven static decision trees. Fragile and unable to handle real-world variations.
   - **ML Classification Systems**: Supervised intent classifiers routing messages into fixed buckets (e.g. order tracking, refund, damage).
   - **Modern LLMs**: General-purpose reasoning agents capable of understanding context, slang, typos, sentiment, and handling multi-turn support interactions.
3. **Core Metric**:
   - Decreasing mean time to resolution (MTTR) while preserving customer trust and answering accuracy.
